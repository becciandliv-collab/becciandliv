Where to find us

Location

Dragon Hall

115–123 King Street
Norwich
NR1 1QE

Dragon Hall is a Grade I listed medieval trading hall on King Street, now home to the National Centre for Writing. It is a short walk from Norwich train station and local bus routes.

Arrival: Please arrive via King Street. We’ll confirm exact arrival timings nearer the day, but guests should allow a little extra time for city-centre travel, parking and walking to the venue.

Accessibility: Dragon Hall is largely wheelchair accessible, with two raising platform lifts, disabled access toilets, hearing loop facilities and quiet spaces. Blue Badge parking is available directly outside on King Street.

Some areas of the historic building may not have step-free access, so please let us know about any access needs when you RSVP and we’ll liaise with the venue in advance.
